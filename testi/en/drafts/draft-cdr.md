# Public CdR table

*Working translation. The Italian text is the authoritative one.*

**Calibration draft 0.2, not adopted.** Code 1 (Art. 46) requires each Community to have a public table indicating the supplements, the limits of the utility recognition, the cap, the decay and the cost in UCR of non-necessary goods and services. It is approved by the Assembly of Circle 1, within the parameters that the General Assembly of Circle 2 calibrates every year (Code 1, Art. 44, para. 4; Code 3, Art. 14, para. 2, letter b). This page proposes a model to start from and checks with a simulation whether the cap and the decay do what they are meant to do: prevent accumulation without emptying the recognition of meaning. Version 0.2 (27 October 2026) brings the draft into line with the rules in force on that date. For an associated Community the CdR and the table are tools: they apply only if it adopts them (Code 15, Art. 21-bis, paragraphs 1, 3 and 4, letter a).

**The answer, in brief.** Yes. With the cap of 240 CdR the most active person cannot get ahead of someone who works a little over three hours a week beyond the Base Contribution, and the most active twentieth of the Community stays below the concentration threshold set by the Code. Without the cap it would exceed it by far. The two points that the texts left open were clarified on 19 October 2026, as set out at the end.

## 1. What the CdR is, in four lines

- It is the recognition of the contribution rendered beyond the Base Contribution (CBO). It is not money: it is not transferred, lent or inherited, and it does not bear interest (Code 1, Art. 42).
- It cannot condition the Basket, the vote, health care, learning or sortition (Art. 42, para. 3).
- It is used for three things only: precedence for non-necessary and scarce goods and services, prolonged exclusive use of a common space, ordinary hospitality in the Bridge Houses (Art. 45, para. 1).
- Precedence applies to at most half of the availability: the other half remains for those without CdR, on the waiting list. CdR that have been used are cancelled and pass to no one (Art. 45, paragraphs 3 and 4).

## 2. How it accrues

These rules are already in the texts: the table summarises them.

| Activity | CdR per hour | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary CBO | 0 | Code 1, Art. 43, para. 4 |
| Civic functions of List D | 0 | Code 1, Art. 43, para. 4 |
| Validated activity beyond the CBO that produces goods or services for the Community | 1 | Code 1, Art. 43, paragraphs 1 and 2 |
| Cultural, artistic and recreational activities in person, open to all (performances, concerts, readings, festivities) | 1 to those who perform or lead, not to those who attend; preparation counts at most as much as the hours in person; no more than 60 CdR a year on first application; no utility recognition; worship activities and those reserved to a part of the members are excluded | Code 1, Art. 43, para. 1-bis |
| Unpleasant, arduous or night task performed within the CBO | the supplement only: from 0.3 to 1 | Code 1, Art. 10, para. 3 |
| The same task performed beyond the CBO | from 1.3 to 2 | Code 1, Art. 10, para. 3 |
| Travel within the CBO, including travel to reach a distant task | 0: the journey counts as CBO hours | Code 1, Art. 43, para. 2; Art. 25, para. 5 |
| Travel beyond the CBO | 1 plus a supplement between 30 and 100%; the draft proposes 30% | Code 1, Art. 43, para. 2 |
| Restorative work | 1, unless otherwise agreed | Code 1, Art. 10, para. 4 |
| Possession of a competence, a qualification or an office | 0 | Code 1, Art. 42, para. 4 |

## 3. Supplements

The Code sets the range: from thirty to one hundred per cent (Art. 10, para. 2). The draft proposes three classes. The list of tasks in each class is drawn up by the Health Council and the Storehouse Council and approved by the Assembly (Art. 8, para. 1).

| Class | Proposed supplement | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Discomfort | 30% | Sanitary cleaning, compost management, repetitive work |
| Heavy work or night | 50% | Physically heavy work, shifts between 22 and 6 o’clock |
| Risk, or several conditions together | 100% | Tasks with significant risk that no robot of the Community can perform safely; heavy work performed at night |

The percentages are drafting choices within the Code’s range.

**Risk and robots.** When a robot of the Community can safely perform a task classified for risk, no person may be assigned to it, except for tests of the manual procedure and breakdowns (Code 1, Art. 8, para. 3; Code 7, Art. 6-bis, para. 6). The 100% class therefore applies only where no such robot exists.

## 4. Utility recognition

For works and services of particular common utility the Assembly may add a reasoned and published recognition, never exceeding the hours actually spent (Art. 43, para. 3). The table must set its limit.

| Proposed limit | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| For each work or service | no more than half of the hours spent | Leaves room for recognition without doubling the value of the hour |
| Per person, in one year | no more than 30 CdR | It adds to the 60 cultural CdR: together they make 90 a year, which at steady state give a balance of 135, 56% of the cap. With 60 and 60 they would make 180, 75% of the cap |
| Who decides | the Assembly, with those who have benefited from the work | Const. 9.2 |

These are drafting choices. They serve to prevent recognition from rewarding popularity more than contribution.

## 5. Cap and decay

| Rule | Value | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Cap on the personal balance | 240 CdR on first application | Code 1, Art. 44, para. 2 |
| How the balance is measured | sum of the current values of the unused CdR, checked when new CdR are allotted; they are allotted up to the cap, supplements included | Code 1, Art. 44, para. 2 |
| Full value | for twelve months from accrual | Code 1, Art. 44, para. 3 |
| Then | one twelfth of the initial value less each month, that is, one quarter each quarter | Code 1, Art. 44, para. 3 |
| Extinction | on completion of the twenty-fourth month | Code 1, Art. 44, para. 3 |
| Who calibrates cap and decay | every year the General Assembly of Circle 2, within any limits set by Circle 3; it may not abolish them | Code 1, Art. 44, para. 4 |
| On death or on loss of member status | the CdR are extinguished | Code 1, Art. 44, para. 5 |
| During the suspension under Const. 7.3 | CdR are neither accrued nor used | Code 1, Art. 44, para. 6 |

**The curve.** The text said “one quarter each quarter” but did not say how. There were three possible readings.

| Reading | Full value for | Extinction | Hours a week to reach the cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gradual: from the twelfth month it declines in proportion to time, one twelfth per month** | **12 months** | **24th month** | **3.1** |
| The whole quarter at the end of each quarter | 15 months | 24th month | 2.8 |
| The whole quarter at the start of each quarter | 12 months | 21st month | 3.4 |

Only the first respects both sentences of the paragraph: twelve months of full value and extinction at the twenty-fourth. It is the one used in this draft and written into Code 1 with [amendment proposal 9](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/proposals.html), accepted on 19 October 2026. The first version of this page used the second, more generous one.

| Months from accrual | Remaining value |
|---|---|
| up to 12 | 100% |
| 15 | 75% |
| 18 | 50% |
| 21 | 25% |
| 24 | zero |

**What it means.** Someone who accrues CdR regularly and never uses them keeps, at steady state, the equivalent of 18 months. The cap of 240 is therefore reached by accruing 13.3 CdR a month: 3.1 hours a week beyond the CBO. Beyond that threshold, working more does not increase the balance.

For the purposes of use only, one CdR **at full value** corresponds to one UCR, that is, to one hour of work (Art. 45, para. 2). The oldest CdR are used first, each at the value it has at that moment (Art. 44, para. 3; Art. 45, para. 3): a CdR accrued eighteen months earlier is worth half a UCR. With the coefficient of the draft UCR Annex, 240 CdR at full value correspond to about 12,000 euros of goods bought outside. In working time they are 16 weeks of CBO at the limit of fifteen hours, but with the CBO estimated by the UCR draft (7.7 hours in scenario A, 10.9 in scenario B) they are between 22 and 31 weeks.

## 6. What non-necessary goods and services cost in CdR

The cost of each good is its real cost: the hours of those who produce it, the share of the means of production and transport, calculated with the method of the [draft UCR Annex](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/draft-ucr.html). One rule applies: an hour is worth an hour, whoever works it and wherever.

- For what the Community produces itself, the hours, materials and energy are counted.
- For what is bought outside, the actual hours are used, where they are known; otherwise the price multiplied by the hours of work per euro of the country of production.

The values are examples to show orders of magnitude: prices and shares produced abroad are assumptions. Each Community draws up its own table. The last column shows what the price alone, converted at the European average, would give: the difference is real work that the price does not reveal.

| Good or service | How it is calculated | CdR | With the European average alone |
|---|---|---|---|
| One kilo of coffee (15 euros) | plantation hours in Rwanda, plus 13 euros of processing and transport in Europe | 3.2 | 0.3 |
| One kilo of chocolate (12 euros) | one fifth of the price in Côte d’Ivoire, the rest in Europe | 0.9 | 0.2 |
| A book printed in Europe (18 euros) | price times European coefficient | 0.4 | 0.4 |
| European materials for a personal work or project (100 euros) | price times European coefficient | 2.0 | 2.0 |
| A musical instrument made in Europe (400 euros) | price times European coefficient | 7.8 | 7.8 |
| The same instrument, half made in China | half the price times Chinese coefficient | 16.7 | 7.8 |
| An appliance beyond the basic allocation (500 euros more) | half the price in China | 20.9 | 9.8 |
| A camera (600 euros) | half the price in China | 25.0 | 11.8 |
| A touring bicycle made in Europe (1,200 euros) | price times European coefficient | 23.5 | 23.5 |
| The same bicycle, half made in China | half the price times Chinese coefficient | 50.1 | 23.5 |
| A 1,000 km train journey | 10 cents per km, European coefficient | 2.0 | 2.0 |
| A 5,000 km train journey | as above | 9.8 | 9.8 |
| One night of ordinary hospitality in a Bridge House (unit cost) | share of 15 m² plus half an hour of management and cleaning | 0.6 | |
| Fourteen nights of ordinary hospitality in a Bridge House | as above | 7.7 | |
| Exclusive use of a common space of 20 m² for three months | 1.28 UCR per m² per year: materials and maintenance | 6.4 | |
| The same space for one year | as above | 25.5 | |

**Spaces and Bridge Houses.** The cost per m² divides the materials and the maintenance hours of the UCR draft by about 27 m² per person: 22 of housing, as Code 1 requires (Art. 47-bis), plus 5 of common buildings. It is an assumption: with the 15 m² of the first version it was 1.66 UCR. The rows on the Bridge Houses concern only ordinary non-essential hospitality. No CdR are required for hospitality for care, emergency, relocation, transit, mobile CBO, functions conferred by sortition or learning, nor for non-confederated persons (Code 11, Art. 6-ter, para. 3; Code 1, Art. 49, para. 3). Between members, hospitality normally lasts at least fourteen days (Art. 6-ter, para. 4): “one night” is a unit cost, not a typical stay.

Four things to bear in mind when reading the table.

- **The CdR gives precedence, not a purchase.** The same goods remain accessible without CdR, by booking or waiting list, and precedence applies to at most half of the availability (Art. 45, para. 4).
- **One CdR at full value is one hour.** A kilo of coffee is worth more than three hours of voluntary work, because it contains three hours of work; a musical instrument made in Europe, eight.
- **What requires more work costs more, not what comes from far away.** Coffee is worth three hours because it is picked by hand, with few means of production. Distance weighs only through transport, which by sea is a small matter: about 20 cents per kilo from China to Italy, less than a hundredth of a CdR. The same good produced with better tools would cost fewer hours, wherever it was produced.
- **No variable prices.** The cost is the reproduction cost, the same for everyone: auctions, markets and exchanges of CdR between persons are prohibited (Art. 45, para. 5).

### Is the cap of 240 right or low?

Compared with costs it is not low: the most expensive item in the table is worth 50 CdR, the cap is almost five times as much. And the cap limits how much can be set aside, not how much can be accrued: those who use their CdR accrue more without limit.

As regards concentration it is close to the maximum that holds. In the Community simulated in section 7:

| Cap | Reached by working beyond the CBO | Share of CdR of the most active 5% |
|---|---|---|
| 120 | 1.5 hours a week | 13% |
| 180 | 2.3 hours a week | 15% |
| **240** | **3.1 hours a week** | **17%** |
| 300 | 3.8 hours a week | 20%: beyond the Code’s threshold |
| 360 | 4.6 hours a week | 24%: beyond the Code’s threshold |
| 480 | 6.2 hours a week | 29%: beyond the Code’s threshold |

The Code requires the cap to be reviewed when 5% of persons hold more than 20% of the CdR (Art. 46, para. 3). In this simulation, at 300 the threshold is just exceeded. A lower cap, for example 120, would make balances more equal but would be reached with less than an hour and a half a week. 240 is a reasonable choice: there is no reason to raise it.

## 8. What can be drawn from it

1. **The cap does its job.** It stops accumulation at a level that a person reaches with a little over three hours a week: those who work much more do not obtain more power of precedence than others.
2. **Decay does not empty the recognition of meaning.** A CdR keeps its full value for twelve months and fades out within two years: there is time to use it, not to hoard it.
3. **Those who perform many unpleasant tasks soon reach the cap.** From that moment the supplement is no longer recognised for them (Art. 44, para. 2). The Code prevents this with sortition and the rebalancing of unpleasant tasks and, for risky tasks, with robots where they can do them safely (Art. 8, para. 3): the table works only if that rotation really works. With the estimated CBO, lower than fifteen hours, the cap is reached later.
4. **The value of the cap is defensible, not proven.** 240 CdR are from 16 to 31 weeks of Base Contribution, depending on the CBO hours. It must be reviewed with the quarterly data that the Code requires to be published (Art. 46, para. 2).

## 9. Two points clarified on 19 October 2026

While writing the first version of this draft, two points emerged that the texts did not clarify. They became two [amendment proposals](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/proposals.html), numbers 9 and 10, accepted on 19 October 2026: the text is now in Code 1.

1. **How the value declines.** Article 44, paragraph 3, said that CdR keep their value for twelve months, then lose one quarter per quarter and are extinguished “at the end of the twenty-fourth month”, but not whether the quarter is lost all at once or little by little. Proposal 9 chose gradual reduction, in proportion to time, the only one that respects both sentences, and added two missing rules: the oldest CdR are used first, and each CdR counts for the value it has at that moment.
2. **What happens to the supplement of someone at the cap.** Article 44, paragraph 2, said that once the cap is reached further activities do not give entitlement to CdR, without saying whether this also applied to the supplement for unpleasant tasks performed within the CBO. Proposal 10 confirmed that it does, and defined how the balance is measured.

## What is needed to adopt it

**Data to be checked, and who could do it.**

- **How many hours beyond the CBO people really work** (today the shares in section 7 are assumptions): the aggregated quarterly data of the first Circles (Art. 46, para. 2).
- **The real CBO hours** (today estimated at between 7.7 and 10.9 a week): the first Labour Balances.
- **Which tasks are unpleasant, heavy or risky, and where a robot can replace people:** the Health Council and the Storehouse Council, with an occupational safety technician.
- **The UCR costs of the goods in the table** (prices, shares produced abroad, country coefficients, coffee, train): the Accounting Council, with real quotes and supply-chain data.
- **The cost of common spaces and Bridge Houses** (1.28 UCR per m² per year): a building technician, with measured maintenance hours and the real floor area of the dwellings.

**Open choices.**

1. **Three supplement classes (30, 50 and 100%).** The draft recommends keeping them.
2. **Annual limits for utility and cultural CdR.** The draft recommends keeping the Code’s 60 cultural CdR and setting the utility recognition at 30 a year, so that the two items together stay below three fifths of the cap.
3. **Share of availability with precedence** (at most half: Art. 45, para. 4). The draft recommends one third on first application, to be raised only if the waiting lists stay short.
4. **Supplement for travel beyond the CBO.** The draft recommends 30%, the lowest class.
5. **Cap.** The draft recommends 240, to be reviewed with the data of the real CBO and with the one-fifth rule (Art. 46, para. 3).

**Approval procedure.**

1. Cap and decay are calibrated every year by the General Assembly of Circle 2, within any limits set by Circle 3 (Code 1, Art. 44, para. 4; Code 3, Art. 14, para. 2, letter b): it is a constitutive decision (Code 3, Art. 3, para. 3), with the majorities of Art. 14, paragraphs 6 and 7. Before Circle 2 exists, the Assembly of Circle 1 or the joint deliberation of the Communities decides (Code 3, Art. 14, para. 10); during the transition Circle 2 is the Basin (Code 16, Art. 1, para. 5). Until then the first-application values of Code 1 apply.
2. The list of unpleasant tasks is drawn up by the Health Council and the Storehouse Council and approved by the Assembly (Code 1, Art. 8, para. 1).
3. The table is approved by the Assembly of Circle 1 within those parameters (Art. 46, para. 1), by the consensus method, after the proposal has been published for at least 72 hours (Code 3, Art. 4; Art. 12-bis, para. 9).
4. The cost column can be adopted only after the UCR Annex, because the UCR cost of goods must be certified by the Accounting Council (Code 1, Art. 20, para. 6; Code 3, Art. 9, para. 5). Supplements, limits and cap can be adopted before.
5. After adoption: aggregated quarterly data (Art. 46, para. 2), review if less than one twentieth of the people holds more than one fifth of the CdR (para. 3), appeal to the Guarantee Circle within 14 days (para. 4).
6. Code 1 is active from Phase 0 (Code 16, Art. 3, para. 1, letter b): the table is needed from the first year.
