# Register of parameters to be verified

*Working translation. The Italian text is the authoritative one.*

**Calibration draft, not adopted.** This page collects the numbers proposed by the texts and states, for each one, where it comes from and what would be needed to confirm or correct it. It does not amend any provision: the values remain those written in the Constitution and in the Codes, and they may become binding only when data exist and the competent bodies are able to approve them.

## How to read it

- **Proposed value:** the number written in the texts today.
- **Location:** the article in which it is found.
- **Origin:** an external source, where there is one; otherwise “drafting choice”, that is, a number set to make the provision applicable, without any data to support it.
- **Uncertainty:** low, medium or high, according to how far the value is supported by data and how much it may vary from one place to another.
- **Evidence needed:** what would have to be measured, calculated or tested.

Anyone who has data, experience or a criticism may [open an issue](https://github.com/ImTheZeroIt/progetto-dei-cerchi/issues/new) or write to [zerocirclesproject@gmail.com](mailto:zerocirclesproject@gmail.com).

## 1. Parameters with external benchmarks

For these values there are data or rules against which they can be compared. The comparison does not confirm them: it shows where to look.

| Parameter | Proposed value | Location | Origin and benchmarks | Uncertainty | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Limit of necessary work (CBO) | average not exceeding 15 hours a week, on a quarterly basis | Code 1, Art. 3 | Drafting choice. A first calculation based on needs ([draft UCR Annex](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/draft-ucr.html)) gives between 9 and 13 hours a week, manufactured goods included. With today’s mode of production, an independent study (McElroy and O’Neill, 2025) estimates about 27 hours by adding the missing services to the bare minimum; the authors point out that none of their scenarios is realistic. | High. It is the parameter on which the balance of the whole system depends. | Actual Labour Balances; measured hours of collective kitchens, gardens and workshops; how much household work is counted in the CBO. |
| Water Basket | at least 50 litres per person per day | Code 8, Art. 4-bis | The WHO (2020) indicates about 20 litres as basic access, 50 litres as intermediate access, with medium health risk, and over 100 litres as optimal access, with low risk. The Sphere humanitarian standard sets a minimum of 15 litres in emergencies and recognises that 50 may be needed in cities. | Low as a health minimum; medium for climate and local uses. | Actual consumption by climate and season; availability of the resource in the territory. | Threshold for a small Community | fewer than 150 persons entitled to vote | Const. 6.5; Code 3, Art. 12-bis, para. 12 | As above: the threshold of 150 has no solid basis. | High. | Verify below what size it is no longer possible to staff all the Councils without overloading people. |
| Quorum of the Assembly | none for ordinary resolutions; 1/3 of those entitled to vote for qualified decisions; 1/2 for those with special guarantees | Const. 35.1; Code 3, Art. 12 | In Vermont town meetings average participation is around 20%: a quorum of one third is higher than open assemblies usually reach. | High. | Actual participation in assemblies of similar size; effect of the republication provided for when the quorum is not reached. [Issue 5](https://github.com/ImTheZeroIt/progetto-dei-cerchi/issues/5). |

| Protected educational reception of minors | no more than 12 months, renewable once; review at least every 3 months | Code 4, Art. 25, para. 9 | Within the range of Italian juvenile probation (messa alla prova, 1–3 years). | Medium | Comparison with educational pathways for minors who have committed offences; test case. |

## 3. Values the texts call for and that are missing

The Codes refer to these values, but no text sets them yet.

| What is missing | Where it is required | References to start from |
|---|---|---|
| Content of the Sufficiency Basket: goods, quantities, units, period | Code 1, Art. 18 | Proposed in the [draft Basket Annex](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/draft-basket.html), with the sources of each quantity. |
| Food Basket: nutritional values and days of stock | Code 9, Art. 4 | The planning figure used by the WHO, UNHCR and WFP in emergencies is 2,100 kcal per person per day: it is a survival minimum, not a standard of living. |
| Energy Basket: guaranteed minimum share | Code 8, Art. 4 | A 2020 study estimates that a decent life for 10 billion people is possible with less than 40% of today’s world energy. It must be translated into a share per person and per climate. |
| Housing: minimum floor area | Const. 1.2; Code 10 | Proposed in [proposal 2](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/proposals.html), version 3: at least a living room, a bedroom and a bathroom; 30 m² for one person, 45 for two, 60 for three. The Italian rule on habitability (Ministerial Decree of 5 July 1975) requires, for a dwelling with bedrooms, 14 m² for each of the first four occupants (10 for each further one) and a living room of at least 14 m²; 28 m² for one person and 38 for two are the minimums for a studio flat. Since 2024 the “Salva casa” decree (Law 105/2024) allows, in existing buildings and under certain conditions, studio flats of 20 and 28 m². The Sphere standard for emergencies is 3.5 m² of covered space per person (4.5–5.5 in cold climates): it is an emergency minimum, not a dwelling. |
| Basic mobility: guaranteed distances and times | Code 11; Code 1, Art. 25 | No reference yet. |
| UCR calculation method | Code 3, Art. 9 | Proposed in the [draft UCR Annex](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/draft-ucr.html), with a worked example. |
| Public CdR table | Code 1, Art. 46 | Model proposed in the [draft table](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/draft-cdr.html). It is local: each Community approves it. |
