# Register of parameters to be verified

*Working translation. The Italian text is the authoritative one.*

**Calibration draft, not adopted.** This page collects the numbers proposed by the texts and states, for each one, where it comes from and what would be needed to confirm or correct it. It does not amend any provision: the values remain those written in the Constitution and in the Codes, and they may become binding only when data exist and the competent bodies are able to approve them.

## How to read it

- **Proposed value:** the number written in the texts today.
- **Location:** the article in which it is found.
- **Origin:** an external source, where there is one; otherwise “drafting choice”, that is, a number set to make the provision applicable, without any data to support it.
- **Uncertainty:** low, medium or high, according to how far the value is supported by data and how much it may vary from one place to another.
- **Evidence needed:** what would have to be measured, calculated or tested.

Anyone who has data, experience or a criticism may [open an issue](https://github.com/ImTheZeroIt/progetto-dei-cerchi/issues/new) or write to [zerocirclesproject@gmail.com](mailto:zerocirclesproject@gmail.com).

## 1. Parameters with external benchmarks

For these values there are data or rules against which they can be compared. The comparison does not confirm them: it shows where to look.

| Parameter | Proposed value | Location | Origin and benchmarks | Uncertainty | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Limit of necessary work (CBO) | average not exceeding 15 hours a week, on a quarterly basis | Code 1, Art. 3 | Drafting choice. A first calculation based on needs ([draft UCR Annex](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/draft-ucr.html)) gives between 9 and 13 hours a week, manufactured goods included. With today’s mode of production, an independent study estimates about 27 hours for a realistic minimum. | High. It is the parameter on which the balance of the whole system depends. | Actual Labour Balances; measured hours of collective kitchens, gardens and workshops; how much household work is counted in the CBO. |
| Water Basket | at least 50 litres per person per day | Code 8, Art. 4-bis | The WHO (2020) indicates about 20 litres as basic access, 50 litres as intermediate access with low health risk, 100 litres as optimal access. The Sphere humanitarian standard sets a minimum of 15 litres in emergencies and recognises that 50 may be needed in cities. | Low as a health minimum; medium for climate and local uses. | Actual consumption by climate and season; availability of the resource in the territory. [Issue 6](https://github.com/ImTheZeroIt/progetto-dei-cerchi/issues/6). |
| Size of the Base Community | as a rule 30–500 persons | Const. 5.1 | “Dunbar’s number” (about 150) is disputed: a 2021 review finds estimates between 16 and 109 with confidence intervals from 2 to 520, and concludes that stating a number is pointless. In Vermont town meetings an average of about 20% of voters take part in towns with fewer than 5,000 registered voters. | High. | Participation data from assemblies, cooperatives and ecovillages, broken down by size. [Issue 4](https://github.com/ImTheZeroIt/progetto-dei-cerchi/issues/4). |
| Threshold for a small Community | fewer than 150 persons entitled to vote | Const. 6.5; Code 3, Art. 12-bis, para. 12 | As above: the threshold of 150 has no solid basis. | High. | Verify below what size it is no longer possible to staff all the Councils without overloading people. |
| Quorum of the Assembly | none for ordinary resolutions; 1/3 of those entitled to vote for qualified decisions; 1/2 for those with special guarantees | Const. 35.1; Code 3, Art. 12 | In Vermont town meetings average participation is around 20%: a quorum of one third is higher than open assemblies usually reach. | High. | Actual participation in assemblies of similar size; effect of the republication provided for when the quorum is not reached. [Issue 5](https://github.com/ImTheZeroIt/progetto-dei-cerchi/issues/5). |
| Minimum autonomy of the Care Nucleus | 72 hours for vital goods and energy | Code 1, Art. 39 | It matches the objective of the European preparedness strategy of 26 March 2025: self-sufficiency of the population for at least 72 hours. | Low as a minimum. | Adequacy for the climate and isolation of the place; stocks actually needed. |
| Validation of precautionary measures | within 24 hours | Code 4, Art. 34 | Stricter than the time limit of the Italian Constitution (Art. 13: 48 hours for notification to the judge and 48 for validation). | Medium: the time limit is protective; it must be verified that a body drawn by lot is able to meet it. | Test case on Code 4. [Issue 2](https://github.com/ImTheZeroIt/progetto-dei-cerchi/issues/2). |

## 2. Drafting parameters

These are numbers set to make the provisions applicable. They have no external source: they must be tested on concrete cases.

| Parameter | Proposed value | Location | Uncertainty | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Care Nucleus | from 1 to 15 persons | Const. 5.1 | Medium | Actual cases of cohabitation and care; effects of the upper limit. |
| Basin | as a rule at least 3 Communities | Const. 5.1 | Medium | Services that a Basin must guarantee (hospital, logistics) and minimum population to sustain them. |
| CBO peak | up to 25 hours a week for no more than two consecutive weeks | Code 1, Art. 6 | Medium | Agricultural seasonality and emergencies: how many peak weeks are really needed. |
| Technical Councils | 3–5 persons, one year, staggered renewal, not eligible to be drawn by lot again before 12 months | Const. 6.5; Code 3, Art. 12-bis | Medium | Continuity of competences with one-year terms of office; suitable persons available in small Communities. |
| Area Councils | three, each of 3–7 persons; interval of 6 months between terms of office | Code 3, Art. 12-bis, para. 12 | Medium | As above, in Communities below the threshold. |
| Administrative time | not exceeding 1/10 of the total CBO, on a quarterly basis | Const. 6.5; Code 3, Art. 12-bis, para. 13 | High | Measure the time that registers, assemblies and Councils really require. |
| Guarantee Circle | 3–7 persons, 90 days (maximum 180 in the year) | Code 4, Art. 4 | Medium | Workload of appeals; time needed to learn the function. |
| Justice bodies | Clarification Assembly 7, Transformation Council 5, Guarantee Tribunal 7 persons; 6 votes out of 7 for extreme measures | Code 4, Art. 27 | Medium | Test cases. [Issue 2](https://github.com/ImTheZeroIt/progetto-dei-cerchi/issues/2). |
| Protective containment | on first application not exceeding 5 years (Grade 5) and 10 years (Grade 6), with review at least every six months | Code 4, Art. 30, para. 3 | High | Comparison with data on recidivism and with legal systems that use fixed-term measures with review. |
| Objection in the Assembly | at least 10% of those voting, within 72 hours of the outcome | Const. 34.2; Code 3, Art. 13-bis | Medium | Test case. [Issue 1](https://github.com/ImTheZeroIt/progetto-dei-cerchi/issues/1). |
| Economic thresholds for decisions | low threshold 100 UCR + 1 per person (at most 500), high threshold 500 UCR + 5 per person (at most 2,500) | Code 3, Art. 7 | High | With the coefficient proposed in the draft UCR Annex, 500 UCR are 0.4% of the annual work of a Community of 500 persons and 7% of one of 30. [Proposal 11](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/proposals.html) links them to the number of persons, with a fixed part. |
| CdR cap | 240 CdR on first application | Code 1, Art. 44 | Medium | A first [simulation](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/draft-cdr.html) shows that the cap keeps the most active twentieth below the concentration threshold. To be reviewed with actual quarterly data. |
| CdR decay | full value for 12 months, then one twelfth per month down to zero at the 24th month | Code 1, Art. 44 | Medium | As above. The text does not say how the value declines: [proposal 9](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/proposals.html) chooses gradual reduction, one twelfth per month. |
| Refusal of the contribution | graduated procedure before the Clarification Assembly | Const. 7.3 | Medium | Test case. [Issue 3](https://github.com/ImTheZeroIt/progetto-dei-cerchi/issues/3). |
| Contribution on external income | 1/10 of net income on first application, never more than 1/5 | Code 15, Art. 19-bis | High | Actual external requirement of a Community in transition (taxes, purchases, health care). |
| Declaration of external income | by bands, confidential, kept for 12 months | Code 15, Art. 19-bis | Low | Compatibility with the tax obligations of the State in which the Community is located. |
| Reserve of the Common Fund | not exceeding 12 months of external requirement | Code 15, Art. 17 | Medium | Variability of external expenditure in the first years. |
| Critical functions | at least 3 trained persons for each | Code 15, Art. 12, para. 7 | Medium | List of the critical functions of a typical Community and training times. |
| Review of privileged access | quarterly, 3 persons drawn by lot | Code 7, Art. 8, para. 8 | Low | Technical verification with those who administer IT systems. |
| Choice of accession | within 12 months of turning 18; silence counts as accession | Const. 26.4 | Medium | Assess whether silence as accession is compatible with freedom of choice. |
| Founding regime | 2 Communities, 6 months, vote within 30 days, 2/3 of persons and 2/3 of Communities, ratification within 24 months | Const. 38.2 and 38.3 | High | Only the experience of the first Communities. |
| Assembly in the first year | as a rule at least one a month | Annex C, Art. 4 | Low | Experience of the first year. |

## 3. Values the texts call for and that are missing

The Codes refer to these values, but no text sets them yet.

| What is missing | Where it is required | References to start from |
|---|---|---|
| Content of the Sufficiency Basket: goods, quantities, units, period | Code 1, Art. 18 | Proposed in the [draft Basket Annex](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/draft-basket.html), with the sources of each quantity. |
| Food Basket: nutritional values and days of stock | Code 9, Art. 4 | The planning figure used by the WHO, UNHCR and WFP in emergencies is 2,100 kcal per person per day: it is a survival minimum, not a standard of living. |
| Energy Basket: guaranteed minimum share | Code 8, Art. 4 | A 2020 study estimates that a decent life for 10 billion people is possible with less than 40% of today’s world energy. It must be translated into a share per person and per climate. |
| Housing: minimum floor area | Const. 1.2; Code 10 | Proposed in [proposal 2](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/proposals.html), version 3: at least a living room, a bedroom and a bathroom; 30 m² for one person, 45 for two, 60 for three. The Italian rule on habitability (Ministerial Decree of 5 July 1975) requires 28 m² for one person and 38 for two. The Sphere standard for emergencies is 3.5 m² of covered space per person (4.5–5.5 in cold climates): it is an emergency minimum, not a dwelling. |
| Basic mobility: guaranteed distances and times | Code 11; Code 1, Art. 25 | No reference yet. |
| UCR calculation method | Code 3, Art. 9 | Proposed in the [draft UCR Annex](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/draft-ucr.html), with a worked example. |
| Public CdR table | Code 1, Art. 46 | Model proposed in the [draft table](https://imthezeroit.github.io/progetto-dei-cerchi/en/register/draft-cdr.html). It is local: each Community approves it. |
