Parameters to be confirmed

These are numbers proposed in order to make the rules applicable. They may all be modified by ordinary procedure. Data and experience that confirm or refute them are welcome. The full register, with the origin of each value, its uncertainty and the test needed to confirm or correct it, is for now in Italian: Parametri da verificare. It is a calibration draft, not adopted, and changes no rule.

Parameter Proposed value Location
Size of the Base Community as a rule 30–500 persons Const. 5.1
Quorum of the Assembly 1/3 of those entitled to vote; 1/2 for decisions with special guarantees (Art. 7-quater) Code 3, Art. 12
Technical Councils 3–5 persons, one year, staggered renewal, may not be drawn by lot again before 12 months Const. 6.5; Code 3, Art. 12-bis
Guarantee Circle 3–7 persons, 90 days (maximum 180 in the year) Code 4, Art. 4
Cap on the CdR 240 CdR Code 1, Art. 44
Decay of the CdR after 12 months, −25% per quarter down to zero at 24 months Code 1, Art. 44
Clarification Assembly / Transformation Council / Guarantee Tribunal 7 / 5 / 7 persons; 6 votes out of 7 for the extreme measures Code 4, Art. 27
Protective containment maximum 5 years (Grade 5) and 10 years (Grade 6), with review at least every six months Code 4, Art. 30, para. 3
Precautionary measures validation within 24 hours Code 4, Art. 34
Water Basket at least 50 litres per person per day Code 8, Art. 4-bis
Founding regime 2 Communities, 6 months, vote within 30 days, 2/3 of the persons and 2/3 of the Communities, ratification within 24 months Const. 38
Reserve of the Common Fund no more than 12 months of external requirement Code 15, Art. 17
Threshold of the small Community fewer than 150 persons entitled to vote Const. 6.5; Code 3, Art. 12-bis, para. 12
Area Councils three, each of 3–7 persons; pause between terms of office reduced to 6 months Code 3, Art. 12-bis, para. 12
Administrative time no more than 1/10 of the total CBO, on a quarterly basis Const. 6.5; Code 3, Art. 12-bis, para. 13
Contribution on external income 1/10 of net income on first application, never more than 1/5 Code 15, Art. 19-bis
Declaration of external income by brackets, confidential, kept for 12 months Code 15, Art. 19-bis
Choice of accession within 12 months of reaching 18 years of age; silence counts as accession Const. 26.4
Critical functions at least 3 trained persons Code 15, Art. 12, para. 7
Review of privileged access quarterly, 3 persons drawn by lot Code 7, Art. 8, para. 8
Assembly in the first year as a rule at least one per month Annex C, Art. 4

The verification on concrete cases required by Codes 3 and 4 also remains to be carried out.
