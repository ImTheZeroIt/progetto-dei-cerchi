Change log

Constitution V.29 and aligned Codes.

Date: 3 October 2026

Subject: revision for consistency and completion of the entire normative corpus (Constitution, Codes 1–16, C.1-Annex A, Technical Annexes A, B and C to Code 16), in two rounds: V.28 (consistency and completion) and V.29 (strengthening of the weak points). Sections 1–6 describe V.28; section 7 describes V.29 and, where indicated, replaces the earlier choices.

Texts of the path: Human History and The Circles Project were not modified in their text, except for the correction of a typo in the latter. Analysis of the Present was reorganised on 4 October 2026: the data are now grouped by theme in eight chapters, with the critical issues and the progress on the same theme in the same chapter, and each figure appears only once; figures and sources are unchanged. The System Against the Individual was revised on 4 October 2026 to remove repetitions: the parts go from ten to eight and the sections from 28 to 25, without changing its theses. The four texts now share the same layout.

1. Criteria followed

  1. The Constitution prevails. Where a Code contradicted the Constitution, the Code has been corrected; where the Constitution was incomplete or ambiguous, the Constitution has been clarified.
  2. The person at the centre, a voluntary collectivity. Every restrictive measure is specifically defined, proportionate, reviewable and never suppresses the vital guarantees; exit is an effective right.
  3. No concentration of power. The Assembly decides; offices are by sortition, short and revocable; the technical bodies do not command.
  4. From need to task. Mandatory labour is only that which is necessary for needs; every automation or saving reduces the CBO.
  5. Technology as an instrument. The AIs propose, persons decide; personal data remain with the person.
  6. Correctability. Every numerical parameter may be modified through the ordinary procedures; the Inviolable Core is protected even against revision.

2. Structural decisions confirmed by the author

Topic Choice
Animals V.28: no working animals, no taking of products. Replaced in V.29 (section 7): respectful collaboration within limits, products only without harm.
Terms of office Two distinct levels: coordination, guarantee and facilitation by sortition for 90 days; technical Councils drawn by lot from among suitable persons for one year, without powers of command.
Exit The Autonomous Coexistence Pact is a right, with land in custody of use; “expulsion” is replaced by removal from the Circles concerned, with the vital guarantees always maintained.
Inviolable Core Completed with the prohibition of concentration of power, the right of exit and the self-protection of Article 37.4; exceptions of Article 1.3-bis made exhaustive.

  • Art. 3: new 3.5-bis (external means of payment only through Common Funds, in the transition).
  • Art. 4: offices (4.4, 4.4-bis), exit and withdrawal (4.8, 4.9, 4.9-bis), Autonomous Coexistence Pact as a right (4.9-ter).
  • Arts. 5–6: size of the Base Community (as a rule 30–500 persons), Basin; new 6.5 and 6.6 on the technical Councils.
  • Arts. 7–8: refusal of the CBO, UCR as unit of account, CdR as personal recognition.
  • Arts. 13, 14, 17, 18, 26, 28, 30, 31: alignments (animals, water, Civic Protection Corps, Care Council, grades of offence, Codes–subjects table, Guarantee Circle).
  • Arts. 34–35: General Assembly as joint deliberation of all persons; double majority.
  • Art. 37: rewritten and numbered (37.1–37.5); Inviolable Core, letters a)–k).

7. Revision V.29 — strengthening of the weak points

7.1 Decisions of the author

Topic Choice
Animals They are free and protected, zero exploitation. They may help, with respect for them and within limits; their products are collected only without any harm.
External income It exists only in the transition; it ceases when the Confederation comprises the whole of humanity.
Refusal of the CBO It remains an offence of Grade 3 (no change).
Other strengthenings All those proposed have been applied.

  • Constitution (V.29):
    • Art. 1.3-bis: “sexual abuse of minors” in place of “sexual acts with minors, paedophilia”;
    • new Art. 3.5-ter on external income and external assets; Art. 3.5-bis referred to the existence of external parties;
    • Art. 6.5: three Area Councils below 150 persons; administrative time within one tenth of the CBO;

    • Art. 18.7: location device only as an alternative to containment, with the consent of the person;
    • new Art. 26.4: express choice of accession on reaching the age of majority;
    • new Art. 31.6: double judgment for Grades 5 and 6 and archive of decisions.
  • Code 1 (V1.5): administrative share in the Labour Balance (Art. 2, paragraph 7, letter e); cost in UCR and scarcity indicator published for every good (Art. 20, paragraph 6).
