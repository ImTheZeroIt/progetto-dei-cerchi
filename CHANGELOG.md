# Change log

Constitution V.29 and aligned Codes.

**Date:** 3 October 2026

**Subject:** revision for consistency and completion of the entire normative corpus (Constitution, Codes 1–16, C.1-Annex A, Technical Annexes A, B and C to Code 16), in two rounds: V.28 (consistency and completion) and V.29 (strengthening of the weak points). Sections 1–6 describe V.28; section 7 describes V.29 and, where indicated, replaces the earlier choices.

**Texts of the path:** *Human History* and *The Circles Project* were not modified in their text, except for the correction of a typo in the latter. *Analysis of the Present* was reorganised on 4 October 2026: the data are now grouped by theme in eight chapters, with the critical issues and the progress on the same theme in the same chapter, and each figure appears only once; figures and sources are unchanged. *The System Against the Individual* was revised on 4 and 5 October 2026: repetitions removed, an “In brief” summary added at the opening and the sections merged into a single, shorter text (from about 3,700 to about 1,900 words), without changing its theses. The four texts now share the same layout.

# 1. Criteria followed

1. **The Constitution prevails.** Where a Code contradicted the Constitution, the Code has been corrected; where the Constitution was incomplete or ambiguous, the Constitution has been clarified.
2. **The person at the centre, a voluntary collectivity.** Every restrictive measure is specifically defined, proportionate, reviewable and never suppresses the vital guarantees; exit is an effective right.
3. **No concentration of power.** The Assembly decides; offices are by sortition, short and revocable; the technical bodies do not command.
4. **From need to task.** Mandatory labour is only that which is necessary for needs; every automation or saving reduces the CBO.
5. **Technology as an instrument.** The AIs propose, persons decide; personal data remain with the person.
6. **Correctability.** Every numerical parameter may be modified through the ordinary procedures; the Inviolable Core is protected even against revision.
